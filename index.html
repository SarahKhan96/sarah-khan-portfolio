About

Curious about why people buy, switch and stay.

I started in business administration at the Institute of Business Management in Karachi and spent two years at Chemli Private Limited, first as a market research assistant and then as a customer insight analyst. There I ran pre-launch studies for new household products, analysed the behaviour of more than 20,000 customers, and presented recommendations to the board of directors.

In 2024 I moved to Germany for an M.Sc. in International Business Economics. Alongside the degree I completed the IBM Data Analyst Professional Certificate and the AWS Cloud Practitioner exam, and I am now working through Stanford's Supervised Machine Learning course.

What I enjoy most is the moment a messy survey export turns into a single chart that changes a decision.
